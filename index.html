What We Offer

Print Services

From quick prototypes to intricate custom pieces — we handle it all. Every print is crafted fresh, on demand.

🧪
Prototype

Prototype Prints

Testing a product idea before going into full production? We print fast, functional prototypes so you can validate your design and dimensions quickly. Ideal for startups, engineers, and product designers.

  • Quick 1–3 day turnaround
  • Draft or standard quality options
  • Multiple iterations supported
  • PLA / PETG materials
  • Dimensional accuracy ±0.2mm
🎨
Custom

Custom Prints

Have a specific design in mind? Send us your 3D file, a reference image, or just describe what you want — we'll design and print it. Figurines, name signs, gifts, and personalised pieces.

  • Customer-provided STL / OBJ files accepted
  • We can design from scratch
  • Any color, any material
  • Personalisation & name engraving
  • Gift-ready packaging available
⚙️
Functional

Functional Prints

Need something that actually works — a bracket, enclosure, fixture, tool holder, or replacement part? We print durable, precision-fit components for home, office, and workshop use.

  • High-strength PLA / PETG
  • Tight dimensional tolerance
  • Thread inserts & hardware supported
  • Post-processing & drilling available
  • Bulk quantities accepted
✨
Decorative

Decorative & Art Prints

Home décor, wall art, vases, sculptures, and showpieces. We dial in the detail so every surface looks clean and every curve is smooth — ready to display or gift.

  • Fine layer height for surface detail
  • Silk, matte & glossy filaments
  • Multi-color prints available
  • Sanding & painting on request
  • Custom sizing available
🌀
Flexible

Flexible (TPU) Prints

Rubber-like, bendable, and tough. TPU prints are perfect for keychains, toys, phone cases, and anything that needs to flex without breaking. Our flexi animals are printed in TPU.

  • TPU filament (flexible & durable)
  • Bend, stretch, squish — won't crack
  • Perfect for flexi animals & toys
  • Soft-touch finish
  • Multiple colors available
🔬
Fine Detail

Fine Detail & Painted Prints

Miniatures, jewellery, tiny figurines, and intricate models that demand precision. We reduce layer height to capture every feature — and offer professional hand-painting for lifelike, high-quality texture and realistic finishes.

  • 0.1mm layer height available
  • Miniatures & scale models
  • 3D printed jewellery & accessories
  • Tabletop gaming pieces (D&D, wargaming)
  • Hand-painted for realistic textures
  • Custom color schemes on request

Our Materials
We pick the right filament for every job — strength, flexibility, look, and feel.
🟢
PLA
Our go-to material — easy to print, crisp detail, safe for home use. Best for toys, decor, figurines, and most everyday items.
Most Popular

PLA Glow
Charges under light and glows in the dark. Perfect for night-lights, glow-in-the-dark toys, room decor, and surprise gifts.

Metallic sheen finish that looks poured from molten metal. Great for jewellery, idols, vases, and display pieces.

PLA Wood
Contains real wood fibres — can be sanded and stained like actual wood. Ideal for rustic decor, frames, and nature-themed prints.
Real Wood Feel

PETG
Tougher than PLA, food-safe, and moisture resistant. Use it for functional parts, organizers, outdoor items, and anything that needs durability.
Food Safe

Classic engineering plastic — heat resistant and impact tough. For mechanical parts, housings, and items near heat sources like car interiors.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before placing your order.

Can I get a custom design?
Yes, we do full custom orders! Send us a reference image or sketch on WhatsApp or Instagram and we'll turn your idea into a 3D printed reality.
What materials do you use?
We use high-quality PLA (rigid, vibrant colours — great for decor, toys, and figurines) and TPU (flexible, rubbery — perfect for flexi toys and keychains) filaments.
How long does delivery take?
3–7 business days across India after print confirmation. We share tracking details once your order is shipped so you always know where it is.
Do you ship across India?
Yes! We ship pan-India via courier. Shipping charges may apply for remote areas — we'll let you know upfront when you order.
What if my product arrives damaged?
Contact us immediately on WhatsApp with a photo of the damage. We'll assess the situation and arrange a replacement or refund as quickly as possible.
How do I place an order?
Easy! Click "Order →" on any product in our catalogue — it opens WhatsApp with the product name already typed. Just hit send and we'll take it from there!
